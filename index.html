Operationalizing AI Governance: Powering Agents and Models with AI-Ready Data

Most enterprises have no shortage of AI pilots. What they lack is a path to production the business can trust. Scaling AI depends on whether the leaders can prove that data feeding AI systems is accurate, governed, and AI-ready.

Join Taryn Stebbins, Community of Practice Leader at Informatica from Salesforce, to hear how IT leaders are turning AI governance from a compliance checkpoint into a way to deliver trusted value faster. We'll look at how a governed data foundation helps teams scale AI with confidence, mitigate risk, and show results sooner.

You'll learn how to:

  • Move AI from pilot to production faster with trusted, AI-ready data
  • Reduce regulatory, privacy, and security risk without slowing delivery
  • Give executives and auditors clear visibility into how AI outcomes are produced
